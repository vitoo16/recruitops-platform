# Stakeholder Source Notes

Primary stakeholder source reviewed for the initial product scope:

`CTV TUYỂN DỤNG ONLINE LUCKY LIFE.pdf` (provided directly by the stakeholder during discovery).

Supported requirements from that source include:
- recruitment posting across Facebook, LinkedIn, TikTok, Zalo, Instagram and Threads;
- collecting candidate CVs/information;
- forwarding candidate information/CVs to the company by email;
- using a structured email subject;
- job openings, sample posts, sample images and commission information being maintained in a shared sheet;
- commissions tied to interview and later employment milestones;
- duplicate-CV commission rules.

These are source-derived business inputs, not necessarily the final generalized SaaS rules.
Any product behavior that goes beyond them must be explicitly designed and approved.
